## Concerns & Recommendations
- **Rendering scope:** static PNGs are fixed snapshots; for interactive exploration (zoom, hover,
  per-class filtering) use `dashboard.html`. Regenerate both after any pipeline change so figures never
  drift from the numbers in `04`/`05`.
- **Readability:** heavy-tailed features are shown 1–99 % clipped (distributions) and on symlog axes
  (outliers) — the raw extremes are real, not artefacts; see `_generated/eda_*.json` for exact values.
- **Recommendation:** promote `dashboard.html` to the shared Google Drive / Notion for Team B & C, and
  include the cross-dataset imbalance + correlation heatmaps in the Week-1 integration slides.
